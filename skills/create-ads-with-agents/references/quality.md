# Quality gates and bounded repair

Use **pass**, **revise** or **unverified** per gate. Give actual artifact/source references, region or timestamp, severity, consequence, owner and smallest useful fix. “Looks AI” and “10/10” are not diagnostic verdicts. A validator checks structure, not truth or creative quality.

## Audience gate

Before ad analysis and generation preparation, inspect the actual research/demo artifact. It must identify a purchase situation, alternatives, desired progress, objection, offer fit, counterevidence, recommended priority and creative implication. Evidence basis, coverage and execution mode must be explicit; the user must see the synthesis in chat. A list of demographics, hooks or tool-availability checks cannot pass this gate. Apply the state.md contract; the helper checks structure rather than evidence truth.

Demo findings remain illustrative or accurately sourced from supplied material. No fake quotations, interviews or inspection counts. A written demo may pass review of its actual text, but cannot pass actual generation or media inspection. Live work may reuse relevant inspected research after an explicit relevance check; recovery of an old provider job does not require repeating research.

## Evidence gate

Check identity/variant, market, dates, source provenance, contradictions and claim scope. Distinguish observed facts, user-provided terms, inference and proposals. An ad's longevity or localization does not demonstrate conversion. A generated product demonstration does not prove the underlying property.

Remove or qualify unsupported spoken, written and visual claims. Verify reviews, ratings, case studies, offers and rights for the assets actually used. See rights-and-wording.md.

## Script gate

Inspect the current production copy/script internally before generation; this does not create a separate concept presentation or user approval stage:
- A current audience gate and traceable link from findings to the specific buyer situation, one supported promise and a credible mechanism.
- PROOF with the required e-commerce portfolio, or QUEST for services, scaled to requested scope.
- Honest offer, exact CTA and consistent destination.
- Meaningful difference between concepts rather than cosmetic hook variants.
- Actual product/identity references, feasible actions and asset dependencies.
- A clear hook/payoff and purposeful visual progression in every video.
- Readable exact copy with realistic speech and display timing.
- No counterfeit proof, fake customer identity, invented result or unsupported implied claim.

A missing review yields a marked blueprint, not a final Review Card. A successful structural check does not replace this review.

## Realism and Anti-Slop gate

The dedicated reviewer inspects actual media against approved references and copy. Evaluate these independently:

**Commercial clarity**
- The buyer situation and offer are understandable without the director's explanation.
- The advertised product/service is recognizable and central.
- Each beat adds relevant information, action, proof, objection handling or the next step.
- The hook connects to its payoff; spectacle does not replace the product.
- Specific language and visuals fit this offer rather than any unrelated product.
- The CTA is explicit, readable and aligned with what happens after the click.

**People, objects and space**
- Faces, hands, anatomy, gaze, clothing and practical acting remain plausible.
- Skin retains natural variation; hard surfaces do not liquefy; material texture and local color stay coherent.
- Product silhouette, seams, closures, hardware, labels and count match the actual reference.
- Objects are supported; weight, grip, contact shadows and reflections are consistent.
- Actions progress through contact and consequence without teleporting or hiding the difficult step.
- Doors, paths and working space are usable; scale and camera geometry are believable.
- Continuity preserves identities, screen direction, locations and end states across shots.

**Design and language**
- The exact headline, price, terms, captions and CTA match the approved copy manifest.
- Typography is contemporary and deliberate, or suitably readable handwriting for a note treatment.
- Hierarchy, contrast, spacing, callouts and placement safe areas work at phone size.
- Generated text has no malformed letters or accidental brands; product marks stay authorized and accurate.
- No false interface state, fabricated message, deceptive button, copied mascot or unlicensed identity remains.

**Video and sound**
- Meaningful action/reveal develops through the clip; a drifting camera over a static idea is insufficient.
- The intended action finishes and the opening pays off.
- Speech is intelligible, natural and correctly attributed; no unwanted words, artifacts or false testimonial.
- Captions synchronize with meaning and remain readable.
- Cuts preserve proof rather than conceal discontinuity; music does not mask speech.
- The final CTA has a stable readable hold and survives platform overlays.
- The ad communicates its core offer and next step when muted.

Realism does not mean adding random pores, dirt, film grain, extreme blur or orange/teal color. A deliberate illustration can be valid when the brief requires it; do not treat it as photographic proof. The default for real people, products and physical environments is believable photographic representation.

## Inspection coverage

Inspect the exported still at full resolution and intended display size. Inspect the entire final video at normal speed with audio, then check important contacts, transitions and text frame by frame where needed. A reviewer must receive usable media, not just a prompt or the producer's summary.

Record separate factual, commercial, realism/design and technical verdicts. If only sampled frames are accessible, report exactly what was sampled and mark motion, pacing and audio unverified. Do not issue an unconditional media pass. If required inspection is impossible, provide the available artifact as an unverified draft and name the missing check.

Technical checks include readable/exported format, measured duration and dimensions/ratio, correct scene order, playback and required tracks. A technically valid file can still fail creative review.

## Repairs and release

Fix the smallest affected layer or shot. Correct exact text deterministically when possible; preserve accepted photography and footage. If identity or geography is wrong globally, fix its canonical reference before regenerating dependent scenes. State the defect and intended change before the attempt.

Allow at most two planned repair passes per affected asset by default, within existing authorization; the user may set another limit. This is a ceiling, not a spending requirement. Keep a cumulative record across resumptions. Stop paid retries when the cap/budget is reached or a needed capability/evidence is absent; deliver useful partial work and the precise issue.

Reconcile uncertain submissions before retrying. Failed jobs may still cost credits. User rejection reopens the relevant gate; preserve prior verdicts and document the new correction.

Release states:
- researched: actual stated research coverage completed, with access limits.
- demo_prepared: a labeled illustrative walkthrough exists; no claim of new research, generation or media review.
- brief_prepared: an explicitly requested text-only brief is complete; this is not a default workflow stage.
- production_ready: current script passed; execution path and required assets resolved.
- generated: actual media exists.
- reviewed_for_test: final media passed all required checks with recorded coverage.
- partial/blocked: useful work exists with a specific unresolved dependency or defect.

None means “proven to convert.” Only actual appropriately interpreted campaign data supports performance conclusions.
