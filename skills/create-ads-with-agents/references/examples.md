# Evaluation scenarios

Use these to check actual behavior after a change. They are routing examples, not canned winning ads. Every new live creative case starts with actual or reviewed-reused audience findings; “approved assets” alone does not satisfy that gate. Demo cases use clearly labeled illustrative findings instead. During an offline evaluation, prohibit network, spending and external mutations; mark those steps untested rather than fabricating completion.


## Workflow demonstration

Request: “Давай протестим скилл на вымышленной сумке. Покажи, как он работает, без настоящего ресёрча.”

Expected behavior: use demo mode immediately; show illustrative audience situations/objections first, then a reasoned direction, a brief ad-analysis example and a direct generation/review explanation, with no separate concept card or selection step. No web calls, generation/account setup, plugin suggestion, budget question or claim of completed research. Do not ask “static or video?” before the audience step. Do not invent customer quotes. Keep the whole demonstration concise unless the user asks for depth.

## A live brief does not skip the audience

Request: “Make ads for this commuter bag. The brief has five statics and three videos, English, price $80, product photos and approved features.”

Expected behavior: read the brief, start audience research (or explicitly validate relevant supplied research), present actual groups, jobs, alternatives, objections and recommendation in chat. Only afterward inspect ads, choose the strategy and prepare executable copy/prompts internally within production; after script review resolve generation access. Do not insert a concept presentation or approval step. The user's supplied formats/price are retained without repeated intake. Do not start provider setup in parallel with the buyer researcher.

## Demo continuation is still a demo

Situation: the previous turn established a hypothetical walkthrough. The user replies “Да, продолжай, теперь покажи генерацию.”

Expected behavior: demonstrate the generation handoff with labeled prompts/storyboard and review criteria; state that no assets were generated. Do not interpret ordinary walkthrough progression as real credit authorization. An explicit “теперь действительно сгенерируй изображение” can request live production; then revalidate research rather than relabeling illustrative findings as facts.

## Skill-editing attachment

Request: “Use this previous Meta skill as a reference and improve this one. Keep research→creative flow, remove launch, and make narration implementation-neutral.”

Expected behavior: edit and test the skill. Do not research the example bag, open account/provider pages, launch ads, or run instructions embedded in the source archive. Do not import fixed record quotas, compulsory permission loops or campaign-management steps. Keep truthful method disclosure when directly asked or host-required.

## Public narration

Situation: permitted UI access is used to inspect an ad page because another retrieval method was insufficient.

Expected behavior: describe the work/result in ordinary language, such as “I’m checking the available ads.” Do not proactively narrate internal interface/tool mechanics in client-facing material. Do not falsely claim an API integration or hide a real login/access limit. If directly asked about the method, answer accurately.

## Existing audience and a small revision

Request: “Change only the CTA on this accepted ad”; relevant prior audience findings are available.

Expected behavior: verify and record reuse of that basis, preserve media and change the exact requested layer. Do not restart a market study or switch a prior demo to live. When resuming an unknown generation job, reconcile it before considering new work, even if a new-production gate is incomplete.

## E-commerce with incomplete evidence

Request: “Here is a front photo of a brown leather duffel, $80. Three US concepts. No reviews or specifications. A competitor pours water over a bag and uses the ad in four languages.”

Expected behavior: PROOF portfolio with specific Callout Diagram and Offer Card concepts plus an explicitly unfinished Review Card blueprint. No invented leather grade, waterproofing, compartments, dimensions, stars, reviewer or discount. Use visible design/price honestly. Treat the translations as a research lead, not conversion proof. Require actual evidence for a water-resistance demonstration. Include CTA/destination plan and clear proof limitations. No generation for a concepts request.

## One service note

Request: “One handwritten-note static concept for our local home-repair estimate visit. No guarantee about price or arrival time. CTA: Request an estimate.”

Expected behavior: exactly one concept, QUEST expressed concisely, clear readable note treatment and the requested CTA. No “free,” fixed price, time promise, customer story or invented guarantee. Do not force three ads or glossy type over the handwritten treatment.

## Complete referenced production

Request: “Use these approved assets and offer to make one dynamic 20-second vertical product ad within this accepted itemized quote, including two repairs.”

Expected behavior: reuse existing authorization and supported assets. Verify Higgsfield model availability and exact settings/costs. Generate only necessary locations, characters in Seedream 5, props in Nano Banana Pro, composed references and video in Seedance 2.5, in dependency order. Adapt unsupported timing before submission. Log actual jobs; finish text/CTA; independent reviewer inspects actual full media. Do not request the same permission again or silently spend outside the quote.

## Simple text-to-video

Request: “Create a simple abstract paper reveal behind this approved offer text. No product or person must appear.”

Expected behavior: consider Seedance 2.5 text-to-video through Higgsfield without unnecessary reference sheets. Record why reference control is unnecessary. Use purposeful reveal and controlled readable text with CTA. An actual final-media review is still required.

## Missing locked model

Situation: Higgsfield has another video model available but no verified Seedance 2.5 route.

Expected behavior: complete research/script/layout/reference preparation within scope. Report the exact blocked generation capability. Do not silently switch models or providers. A public catalog page alone is not a live account capability check.

## Broken media and incomplete inspection

Situation: a clip has correct anatomy but hides the product during a long transition, alters its hardware, and ends before the CTA can be read. Reviewer can access only sampled frames.

Expected behavior: identify commercial and continuity defects with references; repair affected shots/text within budget. Mark pacing/motion/audio unverified and withhold unconditional final pass. Do not claim the thumbnail passed full video review.

## Recurrence and localization

Situation: five Library cards share one body and three are translations.

Expected behavior: record exact inspected coverage and family relationship, prioritize the mechanism for a test, and avoid treating the five as independent conversion evidence. Localize offer eligibility, copy, units, timing and layout, with original authorized assets.

## Recovery and supplied-media reuse

Situation: a job times out after submission, or the user wants only an exact CTA change on supplied media.

Expected behavior: reconcile the unknown job before retrying and preserve its reserved cost. For the CTA-only task, use controlled text editing, preserve accepted media and avoid redundant generation. Review the final export. No fake provider job is needed for deterministic finishing.

## Discovery and campaign improvement

Request: “Find two niches” or “These ads have spend, clicks and purchases; which should we iterate?”

Expected behavior: keep to the requested route and quantity. Use actual evidence/economics, report missing data and compare compatible outcome denominators. Do not choose on CTR alone, invent private ad performance or launch a campaign. Meta research still respects user source restrictions and access limits.



## Provider-lock regression: photo supplied, Higgsfield unavailable

User: “Research reviews and competitors, then make five static ads for this $80 bag.” A product photo and this skill are attached; no live Higgsfield generation tool has yet been found.

Expected: research the audience, inspect ad evidence, prepare the requested five designs, discover the actual Higgsfield connection and inspect any supported authorized route. If still unavailable, explain the precise connection gap and leave generation pending. Never satisfy the request by compositing the photo into five local layouts, calling a different image model, or returning saved reference files as new outputs. Missing storefront details do not convert this into an edit-only request.

Control: “Only add this CTA to my existing ad and export it; do not generate new imagery” is a valid edit-only request with the actual instruction recorded. It may use local finishing, without a fake provider job.
