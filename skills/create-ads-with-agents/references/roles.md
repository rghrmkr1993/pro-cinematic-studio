# Roles and delegation

These are task briefs for actual agents, not permanent background services. The host's native delegation API controls execution; agents/openai.yaml is interface metadata.

## Producer

Own the canonical brief, scope, evidence map, assets, quality gates and budget. Assign only useful independent work, merge results and resolve contradictions through evidence. Record who did each part. Only the producer changes the canonical brief and releases final results. Own the execution mode and audience-first boundary from workflow.md; show the audience synthesis before assigning ad analysis or production. Never delegate a later stage early to make it appear parallel.

## Buyer researcher

This is the first research role in every new creative assignment. Find purchase situations, desired outcomes, triggers, alternatives, objections and returns. Separate exact SKU, comparable-product and category evidence. Include positive, mixed and negative observations, recency, source independence and coverage limits. Return the finding's concrete implication for the ad.

## Ad and funnel analyst

Begin only after the audience synthesis is available and shown. In live mode, attempt Meta Ad Library research and inspect actual accessible creatives and destinations. In demo mode, analyze supplied examples or illustrate the method without a new external hunt. Record ad identity, market/date, hook, proof, offer, CTA, concept family and inspection coverage. Cluster repeated/localized variants. Return useful mechanisms and uncertainty; never infer private conversion data from recurrence.

## Creative strategist

Choose the message and evidence that fit the audience, then hand them directly to production. Do not create a separate concept presentation or selection checkpoint. Apply PROOF and the e-commerce trio or QUEST for services from marketing-frameworks.md. Connect buyer motivation, truthful proof, offer and destination. Meaningful differentiation must appear in the visual execution as well as the title. Use meta-andromeda.md without claiming private algorithm knowledge.

## Director and scriptwriter

Create exact copy, a copy manifest, scene plans and reference dependencies. Keep the product central; define what each action reveals, why the viewer should care, and how the hook pays off. Use image-direction.md, video-direction.md and typography-uiux.md. Maintain identity and realistic physics. A first-person generated presenter is not a genuine customer.

## Higgsfield operator

Activate only in live production after the audience and script gates. Own paid submissions within the producer's authorized plan. In demo mode describe the handoff without provider setup, requests or reservations. Enforce provider/model roles, verify live capabilities, quote, reserve, submit, record IDs, reconcile uncertainty, poll, assemble and provide real outputs. Do not let another worker create duplicate paid jobs. Capability discovery does not authorize spending.

## Evidence and rights reviewer

Review actual sources, claims, script, visible implications and offer terms. Check rights/provenance relevant to used assets, genuine testimonials, material disclosures and CTA/destination consistency. Apply rights-and-wording.md. Return pass, revise or unverified with exact source or artifact references and the smallest fix.

## Dedicated Realism and Anti-Slop Reviewer

Required for final creative review, separate from the author/operator whenever delegation is available. Give this agent the brief, original product/identity references, approved copy and usable actual outputs. Do not prime it with a desired verdict or only the producer's praise.

Use quality.md to inspect both **commercial clarity** and **visual realism**. Check generic interchangeable copy, weak proof, decorative motion, plastic people/materials, identity drift, wrong product geometry, failed contact/weight, impossible space, text errors, poor mobile hierarchy, broken continuity, incomplete action, sound/caption problems and missing CTA.

For each issue return artifact plus region/timestamp, observed defect, commercial or visual consequence, severity, smallest repair, and review coverage. Check an entire video and sound when available; sampled frames cannot pass motion/audio. No numerical beauty score or claimed conversion probability.

An explicitly requested text-only brief or demo gets a critique of its actual written material; do not pretend generated media was reviewed. If independent delegation or media access is unavailable, perform useful direct review, label its limitations and leave inaccessible modalities unverified.

## Optional roles

Niche scout: compare candidates on evidence of spending, demonstrability, differentiation, known economics and operational constraints. Only for discovery.

Experiment analyst: use actual objective, spend, dates, attribution, impressions, clicks, sessions, outcomes, revenue and refunds. Compare like with like and separate attention from business results. Only when results are supplied.

## Handoffs and review independence

Every worker returns its role/task, brief revision, sources, artifacts, findings, contradictions, coverage/failures and recommended action. Keep work task-local and write only assigned files. Researchers do not receive unrelated credentials or submit media, launch campaigns or contact people.

Delegate bounded parallel work within the current stage: for example independent buyer-source searches before the synthesis. Audience research, strategy/ad analysis and production remain sequential; stages with asset dependencies also remain sequential. Keep the team small, usually two or three active specialists within host capacity, inheriting host model settings unless the user requests otherwise. Do not recursively expand the team by default. Review after the relevant draft exists. If there is only one extra agent, it can research first and later review the actual draft with a fresh brief that does not prime a verdict; do not imply separate reviewers or concurrent workers that did not exist.
