# Clean midtones: original ad production examples

These three examples adapt spatial mapping, physical materials, source-color containment and edit discipline from the user's CINEIMAGE v2.7 materials. They are original, unbranded compositions. Borrow the method, not a fixed set, crop, font, lens or color palette. All generation follows the main Higgsfield model policy. Dimensions here are production choices for the stated example, not universal rules.

## 1. Location plate: a small repair-service reception

**Purpose:** reusable setting for an authorized local-service ad. The empty counter is action space for a later approved presenter and prop; it is not proof that the advertiser owns this location. Do not imply it is a real business premises without confirmation.

**Production prompt:**

```text
Create an empty, believable reception area for a small repair workshop, framed for the approved vertical advertising placement. The room has warm-white painted walls, a pale gray floor and a waist-high counter with a matte birch front. It is clean and functional, with mild scuffing near the entrance rather than decorative distress.

View the room from the entrance, at ordinary standing eye height, looking diagonally toward the counter. Keep the counter and rear doorway naturally proportioned, with enough depth of field to read their edges. Do not stretch the space to make it look larger.

On frame left, a narrow window supplies soft daylight. Beneath it stands one shallow closed storage cabinet, separated from the counter by a usable passage. Across the center, the counter occupies the middle third of the image. Leave its central working surface clear and preserve open foreground floor for a later person entering the shot. Behind it, a quiet wall provides a simple background without posters. On frame right, one open doorway reveals a small out-of-focus workbench; its opening remains unblocked and no tools project into the passage.

Diffuse daylight enters from the left and falls across the counter. One small warm task light over the rear workbench creates a contained pool inside that doorway. The wall remains warm-white, the floor neutral gray and the counter naturally pale wood. Preserve detail in middle tones, with deeper values beneath the counter and inside the far room. The task lamp does not tint the reception orange.

Use an observed everyday photographic appearance with clear materials and restrained contrast. No people, faces, silhouettes or human-shaped reflections, including occupants in the rear room or window. No invented business name, signage, certification or written claims. Keep exactly one front counter and one rear doorway. Preserve the clear action area; do not fill it with plants, decorative stools or boxes.
```

**Acceptance checks:** counter scale supports a standing presenter; entry and doorway remain usable; no accidental person; task-light warmth stays local; walls and gray floor retain their color; environment does not suggest an unverified licensed facility. When the final ad adds a person, use the approved Seedream 5 identity reference and explicitly update occupancy.

## 2. Product detail: identity-preserving bag image

**Purpose:** a detail panel for a Nano Banana Pro product/prop sheet, based on an authorized product reference. It depicts visible construction only. It cannot establish leather grade, durability, capacity or a real user's experience.

**Production prompt:**

```text
Create one close product-detail photograph of the authorized bag in Reference A, for the detail panel of its product reference sheet. Use A only for the bag's identity. Preserve its exact visible brown color, seam path, zipper arrangement, handle construction and metal attachment shape. Do not invent an internal compartment, material certification or brand mark.

Place the bag on a matte light-gray work surface. Frame the handle base and its adjacent metal attachment in a three-quarter view, both visibly connected to the same panel. The relevant seam runs through the center of the image. Leave a narrow quiet margin on the right for a later graphic callout. Do not add another loose strap or a second attachment to fill that margin.

Position the camera close enough for the seam and attachment to be readable, with a perspective that does not enlarge the near hardware disproportionately. Keep both features within useful focus; allow the farther part of the bag to soften gently without hiding its construction. The surface supports the bag with a coherent contact shadow.

Broad window light from upper left creates a soft reflection across the brown surface and a narrow highlight on the metal. Preserve the reference's actual surface appearance rather than applying exaggerated pebbled texture or a plastic shine. Keep the gray work surface neutral and the bag brown, with open middle-tone detail around the seam and naturally deeper tone beneath the handle. Do not impose an orange wash or a dramatic rim light.

No people, hands, packaging, labels or generated writing. No beautification that changes seam count, attachment construction or material appearance. Use the authorized source as the identity authority, not a generic bag category.
```

**Acceptance checks:** seam and hardware match Reference A; no extra strap; authentic reference color; visible contact; macro texture does not fabricate a material property; no unsupported caption. Label a callout only with a verified feature. If the source does not resolve the detail, use a wider supported view or request the missing close-up rather than inventing it.

**Small edit example:** “Change only the graphic callout to the approved text ‘See the details’. Preserve the photograph, crop, bag, lighting, seam and hardware. Keep the callout outside the feature area.” This is a graphic text revision, not a request to regenerate the photograph. Follow the precise edit path and [type checks](typography-uiux.md).

## 3. Static ad: a planning note on a real surface

**Purpose:** an original physical-note format for a fictional home-organization service. This is a proposed ad, not a customer note or testimonial. The service and “Explore the service” destination must exist before release. No sample phone number, invented review, guaranteed outcome or competitor branding.

**Production prompt:**

```text
Create a realistic photograph of one pale cream paper note on a lightly used oak kitchen table for a home-organization service advertisement. Compose for a 4:5 feed placement. The note is the primary subject, filling most of the center of the image with comfortable margins. Its top edge tilts slightly away from camera, but the writing area remains almost front-facing and easily readable.

At the left edge, show a small cropped portion of a plain ceramic cup, separated from the paper. Across the center, the complete note rests flat on the tabletop, with only one slightly lifted lower corner and a soft contact shadow. At the far right, place one unbranded capped black pen parallel to the paper's edge. Keep the background quiet; no hands, devices, extra notes or decorative flowers.

Soft morning daylight enters from outside frame left. Paper stays pale cream, the table natural brown and the pen matte black. The grain of the wood is visible but subordinate to the note. Preserve readable middle tones and local material color, with no warm filter covering the page, artificial haze or vignette obscuring its corners.

The note carries exactly these English words, in dark legible casual handwriting, with these line breaks:
Room to reset.
Start with one space.
Explore the service

The first line is largest. Leave clear space between the three lines, and underline the final line once. Do not add signatures, names, dates, quotation marks, icons, phone numbers or any other writing. The lettering has modest human variation without distorted or missing letters. Keep the paper texture subtle enough that every word stays readable at mobile size.
```

**Acceptance checks:** all 10 words appear exactly once and in the correct order; paper remains physically supported; cup and pen do not cover copy; first line and CTA read at mobile size; no fake personal testimony; no unverified promise. If lettering is inaccurate, preserve the accepted photograph and repair the physical text through the authorized precise edit path. Do not keep regenerating the entire scene to chase letter accuracy.

For a language variant, translate and review the copy first, then update only the text and spacing needed for the new line lengths. Keep photography and ad identity stable. If a separate graphic CTA is added, use a contemporary licensed font and a clear visual hierarchy; it must not imitate an operating-system notification or claim to be a real clickable control inside the photograph. Refer to [typography and UI/UX](typography-uiux.md).
