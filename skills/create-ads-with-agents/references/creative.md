# From evidence to executable creative

Begin only after the audience synthesis is recorded and shown in chat; use workflow.md for mode and stage boundaries. Every creative names the finding/job it addresses and the objection its visual support helps resolve. In demo mode these links may be illustrative but cannot masquerade as inspected evidence.

Use marketing-frameworks.md for mandatory PROOF, portfolio types, QUEST and marketing decisions. Use the user's actual scope rather than automatically expanding a request.

## Internal production brief

Translate the chosen strategy directly into executable instructions. Keep this preparation internal during a media task; do not deliver cards, ask the user to choose concepts or add a separate review stage. Show a standalone brief only when explicitly requested. Each creative records:
- Business goal, market, language and placement.
- Buyer situation, motivation, relevant objection and supporting source.
- One persuasion idea and its testable hypothesis.
- Concept family, format and meaningful difference from the other concepts.
- Exact hook, supporting copy, honest offer and CTA.
- Proof available now; missing evidence and claims excluded.
- Visual progression or static hierarchy, with source/reference requirements.
- Real destination or a clearly proposed flow.
- Primary business metric, failure hypothesis and test priority.

For e-commerce, a full portfolio includes Callout Diagram, Review Card and Offer Card. For services, use QUEST and the requested quantity; the three-route portfolio is quiz, case study and direct offer. Missing customer evidence must not turn into synthetic proof.

No invented “winning probability,” simulated ROAS or arbitrary creative score. A useful priority explains the strongest evidence and largest uncertainty.

## Static layout brief

Specify format, crop, primary visual, intended reading order, exact text, callout attachment points, proof source, price/terms and CTA. Choose a treatment suited to the situation: clean product diagram, genuine review, honest offer, readable note or board.

Give photo generation and controlled text finishing separate instructions. Record what may move during a resize and what must stay legible. Use typography-uiux.md. Keep material conditions near the claims they qualify.

## Timed video storyboard

Each scene records:
- Stable scene/concept IDs, intended time range and commercial job.
- Exact action, framing, viewpoint, camera behavior and end state.
- Product/character/location references and continuity constraints.
- Exact speech, sound, captions, price/terms and CTA timing.
- Claim IDs and evidence, including implied visual claims.
- Production method and locked model role, or supplied footage/real UI capture.
- Scene-specific acceptance checks and planned assembly boundary.

State what new information each beat adds. Show recognizable use contexts rather than labeling an ambiguous room “airport” or “office.” Use a clear action, reveal or demonstration; dynamic does not require frantic cuts. Allow an uninterrupted proof action and a readable CTA hold.

Do not invent unseen SKU details or product capabilities from one photograph. If a scene depends on real footage or an internal view, mark that asset required for that scene. Preserve product geometry and physical cause→contact→consequence. Apply image-direction.md and video-direction.md.

Estimate speech timing conservatively, then measure actual audio. Exact duration is a property of the rendered take, not a script-word-count promise. Copy, voice and visual action need room to resolve naturally.

## Asset and generation plan

Distinguish supplied assets, approved generated references and missing inputs. List necessary dependencies in the order location → character → product/prop → composed frame → video, skipping irrelevant stages. Simple text-to-video requires a recorded reason why reference control is unnecessary.

Mark each input required or optional and identify the scenes, concepts or whole project that depend on it. A missing input for one concept need not block independent concepts. Real footage or product detail promised by a script cannot become optional just because it is unavailable; revise the script honestly.

A generated reference sheet supports continuity; it does not make an invented feature true. A synthetic performer may explain a product, but cannot become a real customer's personal experience.

Compile prompts only after the source/evidence and script gates. Follow Higgsfield execution and use the current schema and real bindings rather than fictitious API parameters.

## Funnel continuity

Align ad promise, landing headline, proof, offer conditions and next action. State what exists and what remains proposed. Proposed refunds, guarantees, trial terms, bundles and prices need the business owner's acceptance before appearing as actual offers.

For a service, explain the real process and qualified booking action. For a course, show genuine curriculum or a lesson rather than fabricated grades or earnings. For an app, use accurate UI and a real activation event. For physical goods, show supported construction/use, with truthful shipping/return terms when relevant.

Do not submit forms, create leads, buy products or contact sales merely to inspect a competitor funnel.

## Testing and delivery

Use actual business outcomes. CTR/watch time diagnose attention; they do not establish purchases or qualified leads. Compare attribution windows, denominators, outcomes and downstream quality. Distinguish concept exploration from controlled variants; unequal automated delivery is not a fair A/B experiment by itself.

When results are supplied, distinguish a weak hook, an ad-to-page promise mismatch, offer friction and fulfillment issues. Use meta-andromeda.md to propose the smallest informative creative test; analysis does not authorize campaign changes.

Research output: cited observations, recommendation, uncertainty and implications.
Explicitly requested text-only output: checked scripts/layouts, evidence and asset needs. There is no default intermediate concept deliverable.
Production output: actual final files or clearly labeled drafts, review coverage and remaining limitations.

Deliver no unsupported conversion guarantee. If generation is blocked, provide the ready plan and exact missing capability; do not call it a finished ad.
