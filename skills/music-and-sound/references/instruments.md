# Instruments

Labels: E craft knowledge, F standard. Ranges are approximate. "AI wording" = phrase to drop into a prompt. Check Indian instruments with a native musician for hero cues; AI tools approximate them (U).

## 3. Instrument-to-emotion table (quick pick)
Tender: solo flute, cello, nylon guitar, bansuri. Joyful: pizzicato, ukulele, nadaswaram, kanjira. Grand: horns, choir, strings. Longing: sarangi, oboe, violin. Mystery: celesta, tambura drone, synth pad. Tension: low strings tremolo, taiko, braam. Devotion: harmonium, bells, veena, tambura. Rustic: parai, banjo, thappu, mandolin. Warm trust (brands): felt piano, acoustic guitar, soft strings.

## 4. Layering rule (E)
Lead (1), counter-melody (0-1), harmony bed (1-2), rhythm (1-2), bass (1), texture (1). Fewer layers sound cleaner and give the dialogue room. Short ads: lead, bass, rhythm, one texture.

## 5. Brief wording for humans
Tell a player the pitch (key), register, articulation (legato, staccato, tremolo, pizzicato, gamakas), dynamics (pp to ff), role (lead, support), and sync marks.

## 6. Cautions
- No sample or voice of a real named player imitated in prompts.
- Ethnic instruments are not decoration; match instrument and cultural context (parai in mourning and folk; nadaswaram in auspicious events).
- For hero cues involving Indian instruments, record a real player; AI renderings may mis-tune microtones and flatten gamakas (U).
