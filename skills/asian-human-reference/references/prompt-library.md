## QA checklist (run on every output)

Skin and face
- [ ] Skin tone matches the brief in every frame (no drift lighter, no grey cast from cool light). Compare with a Monk swatch under neutral light; allow natural uneven tone.
- [ ] Texture present: pores, fine lines, small blemishes; not plastic or heavily smoothed.
- [ ] Faces in a group differ in shape, nose, eyes and age; no clone faces or "pan-Asian generic" look.
- [ ] Eyes match the specified type (monolid, hooded, double lid); no automatic double-lid addition; no squint caricature.
- [ ] Hair texture and hairline are plausible, with flyaways and some grey for older people.

Attire and markers
- [ ] One tradition per outfit, correct drape and direction (saree pleats, pallu, dhoti folds, kimono left over right, hanbok jeogori ties).
- [ ] Bindi, sindoor, mangalsutra only where stated; not on men, children or non-Indian characters; not on non-Hindu characters unless a deliberate fashion choice.
- [ ] Turban, patka or kesh handled respectfully and not used as costume; turban wrap looks real.
- [ ] No mixing of religious symbols without a reason; no deities as props.
- [ ] Jewellery, bangles, nose ring match the region and persist across frames.

Behaviour and setting
- [ ] Gestures are real and in context (namaste, bow, wai, head wobble) and not exaggerated or applied to everyone.
- [ ] Eating with the right hand correctly; chopsticks not standing in rice.
- [ ] Background matches the place: signage script (Tamil, Devanagari, Hanzi, Hangul, Thai) readable or blurred, not garbled lookalikes. Garbled script is a common AI error; blur or ask a native reviewer.
- [ ] No poverty, servitude, exoticism or "ancient mystic" framing unless the story genuinely requires it and a reviewer agrees.
- [ ] Gender and role variety (women professionals, men carers).

Video
- [ ] Skin tone, eye shape, jewellery and bindi stay consistent through the clip.
- [ ] Blink, breathing and weight shifts present; no frozen stare.
- [ ] Lipsync matches the actual language; native-accent voice; no mock accent.

Common AI errors to look for
1. Wrong attire mixing (sherwani with saree draping, kimono with hanbok details).
2. Bindi misuse (on every woman, on men, on East Asians, a smear or double).
3. Skin lightened, especially in video or under beauty filters.
4. Generic pan-Asian face repeated across countries.
5. Garbled script on signs and packaging.
6. Sacred items used as props or costume.
7. Hands and finger merges in mudras, namaste, wai.
8. Over-smiling and uniform expressions.
9. Everyone thin, young, and hairless; no elders, no body diversity.
10. Fixed stereotypes: call centre, yoga, martial arts, geisha, street vendor.

Sign-off: a cultural reviewer from the depicted community, a qualified reviewer for regulatory or skincare claims, and the brand owner. This skill and AI output are not a substitute for those reviews.
