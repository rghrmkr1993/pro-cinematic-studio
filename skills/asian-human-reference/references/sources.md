# Sources

Accessed 2026-10-13 through web search and summaries (not full-text reads), so verify any figure before publishing. Everything is paraphrased. Labels: [V] a source supports the claim, [W] weak source (blog, clinic, travel, study notes), [U] unverified.

## Skin tone and measurement
- Monk Skin Tone Scale, Wikipedia: 10-shade open scale by Ellis Monk with Google, released 2023. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monk_Skin_Tone_Scale [V]
- Fitzpatrick scale, Wikipedia: six types, built for sunburn risk in lighter skin; criticised for few dark tones. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fitzpatrick_scale [V]
- Heldreth et al., "Which Skin Tone Measures are the Most Inclusive?" (Google-affiliated preprint). Asian participants rated Fitzpatrick less inclusive than another scale; South Asian validation of Monk not found. https://scholar.harvard.edu/sites/scholar.harvard.edu/files/monk/files/heldreth_et_al._which_skin_tone_measures_are_the_most_inclusive_vpreprint.pdf [V, partly read]
- "Beyond Skin Tone: A Multidimensional Measure of Apparent Skin Color", arXiv 2309.05148 (2023). https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.05148 [V, summary only]

## Lighting skin on camera
- Ava Berkofsky ASC (Insecure), PetaPixel, 2017-09-14: expose for the skin, reflective skin, large soft sources, no fixed colour formula. https://petapixel.com/2017/09/14/look-hbos-insecure-lights-black-actors/ [V, summary]
- Mic interview on the same subject (date unconfirmed). https://www.mic.com/articles/184244/keeping-insecure-lit-hbo-cinematographer-ava-berkofsky-on-properly-lighting-black-faces [W]
- Gap: no source on Indian or Asian cinematographers lighting South Asian skin was found; the Santosh Sivan result was biographical only. [U]

- Jack, Caldara and Schyns (2012), PNAS, East Asian and Western expression models differ in eyes versus mouth. https://www.pnas.org/doi/pdf/10.1073/pnas.1200155109 [V, summary]. The title says the expressions are "not culturally universal".
