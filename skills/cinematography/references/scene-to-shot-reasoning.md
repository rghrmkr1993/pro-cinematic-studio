# Scene to shot reasoning
Run this whenever the user describes a scene, before any shot list or prompt. Output the answer to each step in one line, then the shot rows. Labels: (K) craft, (V) vendor, (H) house rule. Other references hold the detail; this file holds the order of thought. Never skip step 13; credits are spent only after it passes.

## The protocol
1. **Story intent and emotion.** Write: who wants what, what changes, what the viewer should feel (one emotion word, one secondary). No change, no shot. If the scene is unclear, ask one question before designing.
2. **Audience and platform.** Cinema, YouTube, Reels or Shorts (no TikTok for Indian clients), ad, OTT. Sets aspect ratio (see ratio table in `exposure-and-settings.md`), pace and safe margins. Vertical means tight, centred, big faces.
3. **Format or camera look.** Pick one: clean digital (Alexa 35, Venice 2 style), 35mm film, 16mm or Super 8, phone, IMAX scope, animation. Use `camera-systems.md`. Remember body names are style tokens, not simulations (no controlled test exists, see `camera-systems.md`).
4. **Shot size and angle per beat.** One row per story beat, each different in size or angle from its neighbour (30-degree or two-size-step rule). Use `shot-sizes-and-angles.md`. Wide to establish, medium to relate, close to feel, insert to prove.
5. **Lens, aperture, depth of field.** Wide for space and tension, long for intimacy and isolation, f/1.4 to isolate, f/11 for clarity. Use `lens-deep.md`. Keep hardware in the Cinema Studio panel when available. (V)
6. **Movement and speed.** Lock off unless motion carries meaning. One move per shot, with speed and start and end framing. Use `camera-movement.md`. Chase or fear may justify handheld; calm, awe and luxury take slow gimbal, dolly or crane.
7. **Shutter, frame rate, motion blur.** 24 fps and 180-degree shutter by default; 25 fps for Indian broadcast delivery (confirm the platform spec; see `frame-rates-deep.md`). Narrow shutter for violence and urgency, slow motion (120 fps look) for grace and impact. Words only; models ignore numbers. See `exposure-and-settings.md`.
8. **Light.** Name key (source, direction, quality, Kelvin), fill or ratio, back or rim, practicals, time of day, haze. Motivated sources only. See `lighting-for-film.md`. Check skin tone care for the actual cast. (H)
9. **Colour and grade.** Three-colour palette 60:30:10, stock or LUT name (one), grain and halation level. See `color-and-look.md`. Meaning is cultural; confirm for the audience.
10. **Composition and blocking.** Eye path, thirds, depth layers, headroom and lead room, screen direction and the 180-degree line. See `composition-and-blocking.md`.
11. **Realism cues.** Imperfect skin texture, breath or sweat, dust, wet reflections, practical flicker, fabric movement, correct signage and cultural details, light bounce on faces, not shiny. Use `asian-human-reference` for Indian faces and `photoreal-humans` for texture. Add grain, drop "8k ultra detailed".
12. **Translate to the model dialect.** Assemble: `[Size + angle + lens] + [Subject and one action, start to end pose] + [Environment, 3 anchors] + [Camera move, speed, start and end framing] + [Light] + [Look] + [Constraints]`. Use positive phrasing. Then adapt per model: Seedance (one move, push-in safest), Kling (clause separation, partial orbit), Veo (cinematography first, first and last frame, audio tags), Runway (plain English, no "then"). See `ai-camera-prompting.md`. Keep to about 450 characters when Elements consume the limit (512 cap is a 3P claim for Cinema Studio 2.5 and 3.0; 4.0 has no published cap, see `prompt-compression.md`).
12b. **512 check.** Save the final prompt to a file and run `python -c "import sys;print(len(open(sys.argv[1],encoding='utf8').read().strip()))" prompt.txt`. Pass is 512 or fewer (minus about 90 per @ chip in Cinema Studio 2.5). Over budget: compress with the slot method in `prompt-compression.md`, or split into timed beats. Log the count in the shot row. (H)
13. **Self-critique (10 questions)** before credits are spent. Fail any, fix, re-ask.
 1. Does each shot carry a story change? 2. Is the emotion visible in size, light and colour, not only in words? 3. Is there one move, one action and one key light? 4. Is the lens and aperture consistent with the intimacy asked? 5. Does the cut from the previous shot change size or angle enough? 6. Are screen direction and eyelines continuous? 7. Is the light motivated and the skin tone treated with care? 8. Does the palette hold across the sequence and avoid a clash with the culture? 9. Could the model actually render this (hands, text, crowds, fights, long takes)? (see failure table in `ai-camera-prompting.md`) 10. Is the draft cheap, with a token and credit estimate quoted before go? (H)

## Decision table: emotion to craft
| Emotion | Lens and size | Angle | Movement | Light | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Love, tenderness | 65 to 100mm, MCU to CU, f/1.8 | Eye level, slightly low on her | Slow push-in, gentle drift | Soft golden backlight, wrap fill, 3500 to 4500K | Warm amber, rose, soft pastel |
| Desire, luxury | 85 to 100mm macro and long, shallow | Low and glide | Slow dolly or orbit | Rim, edge strips, dark gradient | Black, gold, deep neutral |
| Awe, scale | 14 to 28mm anamorphic wide | High or very low wide | Crane up, helicopter, slow push | Hard side sun, haze, backlit mist | Rich earth, gold, deep sky |
| Fear, dread | 24 to 35mm wide, long lens for stalking | Slightly low or Dutch off axis | Slow creep or locked | Underexposed, one practical, hard shadow | Sick green, cold blue, black |
| Tension, chase | 24 to 35mm handheld, 135mm compression | Low and behind | Handheld follow, whip pan | Mixed sodium and neon, wet reflections | Teal and amber, high contrast |
| Joy, celebration | 24 to 35mm, f/4 | Eye level, slightly high wide | Gentle crane, orbit, handheld energy | Bright, warm, saturated, practical garlands | Saturated gold, red, magenta |
| Sadness, loss | 50 to 85mm, long hold | Eye level or high looking down | Locked, very slow pull-back | Cool window, low contrast | Desaturated blue, grey |
| Power, authority | 35 to 50mm | Low angle | Slow push, static symmetry | Hard top, rim | Steel, navy, deep red accent |
| Isolation | 24 to 35mm with subject small, wide space | High or distant | Locked, slow pull | Flat overcast or single window | Muted cool, negative space |

## Worked examples
Each: scene, then steps 1 to 12, then the prompt. Shot IDs follow `shot-list-templates.md`. Prompts hold hardware in the UI; the settings line says what to set.

### 1. Tamil village dawn love scene
Scene: "A young couple meet at a village well at dawn."
1. Intent: shy first spark; she lingers, he notices; tenderness, hope. 2. Platform: OTT or YouTube film, 2.39:1 (Reels cut 9:16 later). 3. Format: 35mm film look, Kodak Vision3 250D feel, Indian realist register. 4. Beats: S01-SC01-SH001 wide establish (eye level); SH002 medium two-shot over her shoulder; SH003 CU her glance (slightly low); SH004 CU his reaction. 5. Lens: 28mm wide, then 85mm f/1.8 for the CUs. 6. Move: wide locked with mist drift; CU slow push-in 3 s. 7. 24 fps, 180-degree shutter, no slow-mo. 8. Light: low sun behind her at 10 degrees, 3800K rim and warm bounce from a whitewashed wall, cool blue sky shadow 7000K, ground mist haze. 9. Palette: terracotta, sage, marigold accent; subtle grain. 10. Blocking: well on the left third, she walks toward lens-right; he enters from the right so they face; keep the line fixed. 11. Realism: wet earth, brass pot with real water sheen, saree cotton texture, deep-brown skin with warm highlights, no gray cast. 12. Model: Seedance or Kling, one move.
Prompt SH003: "Close-up, slightly low angle, 85mm shallow focus. A young Tamil woman in a cotton saree pauses at a village well and glances right, a small smile forming. Terracotta wall, brass pot, rising mist. Slow push-in over 4 seconds, then hold. Low golden sun behind her, 3800K rim on her hair, soft warm bounce, cool blue shadows. Warm film grain, natural deep brown skin, locked horizon. One continuous shot."
Settings: 35mm Film, Warm Vintage lens, f/1.4 for CUs; tempo Calm.

### 2. Luxury skincare product hero
Scene: "A premium face serum bottle, hero shot." (no brand name; use the client's own asset via reference image)
1. Intent: desire and trust; calm purity. 2. Platform: Reels and product page, 9:16 plus 16:9 variants; 4:5 stills. 3. Format: clean digital full frame with macro. 4. Beats: SH001 hero front low angle; SH002 macro dropper bead; SH003 pull-out hero on stone. 5. Lens: 100mm macro f/4 for the bead, 85mm f/5.6 for hero. 6. Slow dolly in, 5 s. 7. 24 fps look; one 120 fps look for the droplet. 8. Light: large softbox top, two strip boxes for glass edges, backlight through amber liquid, black flag for edge contrast, dark gradient. 9. Palette: cream, deep amber, one gold accent; neutral white balance, no teal cast. 10. Bottle on left third, copy space right; symmetrical. 11. Realism: contact shadow, thin reflections, condensation, true label (use reference, never invent text). 12. Model: Seedance image-to-video with the hero frame.
Prompt SH002: "Extreme close-up, eye-level, 100mm macro. A single golden serum bead swells at the tip of a glass dropper, then falls in slow motion, 120fps look. Pale stone surface, soft shadow, shallow focus. Slow dolly in over 4 seconds. Backlit amber glow, strip-light edges on glass, soft gradient background. Clean premium grade, subtle grain, neutral whites."
Settings: Modern body, Clean Sharp lens, f/4. Claims about results stay with compliance; no before and after.

### 3. Rainy Chennai night chase
Scene: "A man runs through Chennai streets at night in rain, chased."
1. Intent: panic, tension; survival. 2. Platform: OTT 2.39:1; hook cut 9:16. 3. Format: digital, sodium and neon, anamorphic. 4. Beats: SH001 wide alley (low); SH002 handheld follow behind; SH003 long-lens crowd compression of the pursuers; SH004 CU face, breath; SH005 whip to a bus passing. 5. Lens: 24mm wide, 135mm compression, 35mm follow, f/2 look. 6. Handheld, tempo Chaotic, one move per clip. 7. Narrow shutter (90 degrees) for crisp rain, one 60 fps look. 8. Light: 2200K sodium pools, magenta and cyan shop signs, rain backlit, wet asphalt reflections, blue ambient moon base. 9. Palette: teal, sodium amber, red tail lights; light halation on signs. 10. Cross the road left to right every shot; pursuers right to left; the 180 line follows the road. 11. Realism: wet hair, ankle splashes, breath fog (humid, not cold, so only subtle), auto-rickshaw, Tamil signage placeholder text avoided or from reference. 12. Model: Seedance (action cap), avoid close fights.
Prompt SH002: "Medium shot from behind, 35mm. A man in a soaked grey shirt sprints down a narrow Chennai lane at night, glancing back once. Wet asphalt, sodium streetlights, neon signs, parked autos. Handheld follow at running speed, steady. Backlit rain, 2200K sodium glow, magenta and cyan sign spill, subtle halation. Crisp rain, fine grain, teal and amber grade. One continuous shot."
Settings: DV Camcorder or Modern body, Anamorphic lens, tempo Chaotic.

### 4. Temple festival wide
Scene: "A South Indian temple festival with a chariot and a crowd."
1. Intent: awe, devotion, joy. 2. Platform: YouTube or festival film 2.39:1; 16:9 deliverable. 3. Format: large format anamorphic. 4. Beats: SH001 drone-high wide reveal; SH002 eye-level procession wide; SH003 long-lens crowd faces; SH004 insert of lamps and flowers; SH005 CU devotee. 5. Lens: 24mm wide, 135mm compression for SH003, 100mm macro for lamps. 6. Crane up or helicopter reveal for SH001, slow; others locked. 7. 24 fps, 180-degree shutter; 120 fps look on flower-throw only. 8. Light: late afternoon golden sun from the right, 3500K, dust and incense haze, oil lamp practicals, flares. 9. Palette: gold, red, saffron; deep temple stone and blue sky; rich saturation. 10. Gopuram anchors top-left third; procession moves left to right throughout. 11. Realism: varied faces, lungis, sarees, flower garlands, offerings, no sacred imagery altered or mocked; avoid readable fake text. 12. Model: Veo or Kling for the wide; Seedance for inserts; accept crowd limits.
Prompt SH001: "Aerial high wide, 24mm anamorphic. A wooden temple chariot is pulled by a dense crowd through a South Indian street below a gopuram, garlands and flags swaying. Slow crane up and forward over 6 seconds revealing the full procession. Golden late sun from camera right, dust and incense haze, soft horizontal flare. Rich saffron, gold and red, fine grain. One continuous shot."
Settings: 35mm Film, Anamorphic, f/11; genre Epic; tempo Calm. Respect cultural and religious sensitivities; consult the client.

### 5. Corporate office drama
Scene: "A manager tells a senior employee that the team is being cut."
1. Intent: controlled conflict; guilt vs defensiveness. 2. Platform: YouTube, OTT 16:9 or 1.85:1. 3. Format: clean digital full frame, Zeiss-like neutral prime. 4. Beats: SH001 wide glass office (static); SH002 OTS on manager, 50mm; SH003 OTS reverse; SH004 CU employee; SH005 insert of papers. 5. Lens: 35mm wide, 50 to 85mm for OTS and CU, f/2.8. 6. Locked, slow 3-second push on the CU when the line lands. 7. 24 fps, 180-degree shutter. 8. Light: cool 5600K window hard side, overhead panels, desk lamp warm practical on manager, 3:1 ratio, low haze. 9. Palette: steel, glass, one warm accent (lamp); minimal grade, light grain. 10. Manager screen left facing right; employee screen right facing left; do not cross the line; city skyline behind. 11. Realism: crumpled shirt, coffee ring, subtle breath, a lit laptop; avoid readable screen text. 12. Model: Veo or Seedance, dialogue short; audio recorded separately.
Prompt SH004: "Close-up, eye level, 85mm shallow focus. A middle-aged Indian man in a pressed blue shirt listens at a glass-walled desk, his jaw tightening, eyes lowering. Blurred open office behind. Slow push-in over 3 seconds, then hold. Cool window key from camera left, 5600K, soft warm desk lamp fill, subtle haze. Muted steel grade, fine grain, natural skin texture."
Settings: Modern body, Clean Sharp, f/4; genre Drama.

### 6. Animated fantasy kingdom
Scene: "A floating kingdom at sunrise, a young princess runs along the wall."
1. Intent: wonder, courage; light, bright adventure. 2. Platform: YouTube or kids channel 16:9; Reels 9:16 cut. 3. Format: stylised 3D or painted animation, not a real camera body; use "virtual camera". 4. Beats: SH001 grand wide establish; SH002 tracking side-on run; SH003 low reveal; SH004 CU wonder. 5. Lens: 18mm virtual wide for scale, 50mm for faces, deep focus f/8 look; shallow for CU. 6. Crane up, tracking, push-in; one per shot. 7. 24 fps, soft motion blur; hold key frames for animation snap. 8. Light: sunrise rim, rose and gold sky, volumetric shafts, luminous glow on towers. 9. Palette: jewel teal, rose gold, ivory; no teal and orange default. 10. Castle on right third, princess runs left to right; foreground banners, middle wall, background clouds. 11. Realism cues become believability: cloth sway, hair, particles, parallax, consistent character sheet; keep proportions. 12. Model: Seedance 3D or Pixar-style pack (`02-3d-cgi`) or `03-cartoon`.
Prompt SH002: "Side tracking shot, medium wide, 35mm virtual lens. A young princess in a cream cloak runs along a floating castle wall, cloak flying, clouds streaming below. Camera tracks beside her at running speed, steady. Warm sunrise rim light, rose and gold sky, soft volumetric shafts. Painterly 3D animation, jewel palette, soft depth of field. One continuous shot."
Settings: Genre Epic or Comedy to taste; lens Clean Sharp; keep character as an Element.
