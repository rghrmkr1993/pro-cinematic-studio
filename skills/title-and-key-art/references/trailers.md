# Teasers and trailers

Labels: (3P) secondary, (E) estimate, (A) assumption. Sources are tutorials and industry blogs; treat timings as conventions, not rules.

## 1. Lengths and roles
| Piece | Length | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Social spot | 15 s | One hook, title, date; 9:16 first |
| Teaser | 30 s (up to 60) | One image, one question, title; mood over plot |
| Trailer | 60 s | Premise and stakes in a compact arc |
| Full trailer | 2 min (cinema norm since 2014, 3P) | Setup, turn, escalation, button |
A campaign set: teaser, one full trailer, 15 and 30 s spots, character or scene clips (E).

## 2. Structures
Teaser, 30 s, two acts (3P): context and mood (0 to 15 s), one set piece or line (15 to 25 s), a silence beat, title and date (25 to 30 s).
15 s (E): hook shot 0 to 3 s (mid-motion), two escalating shots, title card at 11 to 13 s, date or "coming soon" in the last 2 s.
60 s (E): cold open 0 to 8 s, premise 8 to 25 s, turn at about 25 s (smash cut or silence), escalation 25 to 48 s, title at 48 to 52 s, button and date 52 to 60 s.
2 min (3P):
1. Setup, 30 to 45 s: world and lead, holds of 2 to 4 s, sparse music, dialogue carries the premise.
2. Turn and escalation, 45 to 60 s: the inciting event on a smash cut or a drop to silence; the score shifts; cuts shorten.
3. Climax montage, 30 to 45 s: rapid cuts to the music hits, the title at the loudest point after a beat of silence (placement varies), then a button (a joke, scare or line) and the release card.
Text cards: 2 to 4 cards, a few words each, placed between beats.

## 3. Cutting method
1. Write the trailer's promise in one line; choose 12 to 25 approved shots from the film; show only what the film delivers, and label concept shots.
2. Build the music first: temp track, mark hits and drops; a custom or licensed track only (originals; no copyrighted score without a licence). Use music-and-sound for the cue.
3. Cut on the beat grid with edit-director (beat-sync) or ffmpeg; shorten holds as the trailer escalates.
4. Dialogue: 2 to 4 lines, strongest first; subtitles burned in for silent social viewing (libass for Indic).
5. Sound design: risers, hits, silence, whooshes; mix to -14 LUFS with true peak at or below -1 dBTP for web unless the platform says otherwise (the editor's rule).
6. Title card and logos from title-designer; end card with date, platforms, and rating only if certified (CBFC mark rules: confirm).
7. Versions: 16:9 master, 9:16, 1:1; clean and captioned; with and without the end card.
8. QA and sign-off as for the film (creative-studio scorecard, 90 for finals).

## 4. Honesty and rules
- AI disclosure per platform (the YouTube altered or synthetic content setting for realistic content; see film-legal-distribution).
- Certification and rating marks only after the certificate exists.
- No spend, upload or posting without the user's yes.
