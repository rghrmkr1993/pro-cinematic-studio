# CBFC certification (India)

Not legal advice. Labels: (F) official or primary-linked, (3P) news, law-firm or exam-prep summary, (E) estimate, (A) assumption, (U) unverified. The portal and the official rules are the authority; confirm everything on the live CBFC site and e-Cinepramaan before filing. This extends film-studio delivery-and-legal.md section 8.

## 1. Legal basis
- Cinematograph Act 1952 as amended in 2023; Cinematograph (Certification) Rules 2024, notified 15 March 2024 (G.S.R. 214(E), MIB), superseding the 1983 rules (3P summaries; read the gazette text).
- Certificates are valid in perpetuity under the 2023 amendment (3P). Re-certification is needed to change category or to move a film to television or another medium, especially for A and S categories (3P).

## 2. Who needs it
Any film exhibited in a cinema in India needs a CBFC certificate. Online platforms (OTT, YouTube) are governed by intermediary and digital media rules and platform policies, not a CBFC certificate in the same way (3P; counsel to confirm per platform). Many OTT services ask for a content rating of their own or a self-classification. A festival may not need a CBFC certificate for a screening but may require a special exemption or permission for foreign or non-certified films (A: ask the festival).

## 3. Categories (3P)
| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| U | Unrestricted public exhibition, all ages |
| UA 7+ | Parental guidance advised under 7 |
| UA 13+ | Parental guidance advised under 13 |
| UA 16+ | Parental guidance advised under 16 |
| A | Adults only (18 and over) |
| S | Specialised audiences, such as doctors or scientists |
Notes: the UA age markers are recommendatory for parents or guardians (3P). One outlet printed "12+" in a headline while the notification text and most sources say 13+; use the gazette text (U for that outlet).
Typical content factors: violence, sexual content and nudity, language, drugs, tobacco and alcohol warnings, religion and community sensitivity, themes disturbing to children. Plan the cut toward the category you want before the DCP is made.

## 4. Process (3P unless noted; confirm on the portal)
1. Register the producer or applicant on the CBFC online system (e-Cinepramaan) and get a producer ID (A: details on the portal).
2. Prepare the dossier: Common Application Form; synopsis; full script or continuity including complete lyrics; list of cast and crew and credits; format details (film length, language, format, runtime); music and rights authorisations; language versions and subtitles; fee payment; applicant's declaration (3P).
3. Submit the application online (Rule 22 requires the portal for applications for public exhibition and for re-certification) (3P). It is routed through the Regional Office per the First Schedule (3P).
4. Film submission: file or DCP as the portal and regional office specify (A). Subtitles in English are normally required for non-English films (A).
5. Examination: the examining committee views the film and may recommend cuts or a category; the applicant can accept, revise and resubmit, or appeal (3P).
6. Fee and timelines: the rules set a target of 48 working days (3P); CBFC reported averages of about 18 working days for features and 3 for shorts (PIB Feb 2026 as cited in film-studio, 3P). A priority scheme with a higher fee and a short examination window exists (3P: 3 times the fee, about 5 days; U).
7. Certificate issued: the certificate and category are displayed on the film; trailers and promos also need appropriate certification for theatre (A).
8. Accessibility: films must incorporate accessibility features for persons with disabilities to be certified (3P); plan captions, audio description or subtitled versions and ask the regional office what format.
9. Statutory warnings: tobacco, alcohol and drug warnings as the rules require (A: confirm scope).
10. Appeals and revision: to the revising committee or the appellate route as set in the rules; High Court after that (3P, U for the current appeal body).

## 5. AI use and CBFC
- The sources read for this skill (2026-10-14) do not show any CBFC rule or form field that requires an AI declaration for certification (3P, absence of evidence; U).
- The general AI rules in India sit in the IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Amendment Rules 2026, notified 10 Feb 2026, in force 20 Feb 2026: they cover "synthetically generated information", require platforms to label such content, collect user declarations for large platforms and embed provenance where feasible; MeitY enforces via intermediary safe harbour (3P law-firm summaries). They aim at platforms, not film certification; whether and how they affect a theatrical film is unaddressed in the sources (U).
- Our approach: declare AI use truthfully wherever any form asks for production details or credits; list AI roles in credits; keep an AI-use statement ready; ask the regional office, in writing through the producer or counsel, whether a declaration is expected; record the answer in the rights file.
- Deepfakes of real persons are a legal risk independent of certification (personality rights, defamation, the IT Rules): no real-person likeness without consent.

## 6. Dossier checklist (producer)
Applicant registration; Common Application Form; synopsis; script with full lyrics; credits list; runtime and format sheet; DCP or file as required; English subtitles; music licence summary and cue sheet; consent and rights summary; language version list; accessibility plan; fee receipt; previous certificates if any; contact and signing authority; a calendar with the release date and a 2x buffer on the stated examination times.
Lawyer reviews the declaration and the rights statements before filing; the user files or approves the filing.

## 7. Planning notes
- Target category: decide in pre-production; the story and the shoot decide it.
- Cuts: keep a separate cut list for CBFC with timecodes; any change after certification can need re-examination (A).
- Trailer certification and poster rating marks follow the certificate.
- Festival or online-only plan: state it, so a certificate is not applied for needlessly.
