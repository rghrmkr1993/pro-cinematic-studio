## 2. Likeness and consent
- Real people: written release covering AI generation, scope, media, territory, term, voice, right to revoke; keep records. Soul ID and voice clones only for yourself or consenting people (V). No celebrities, no public figures, no minors.
- Deepfakes or likeness without consent are banned by ASCI regardless of label and covered by personality-rights case law in India (3P).
- Reference ads: only with rights; real people appearing in a reference ad need consent (V Ad Multiplier note).
- Original AI characters are acceptable but must be disclosed where realistic.

## 3. Testimonials and endorsements
- No fabricated customers, doctors, dermatologists or "results" people. ASCI bans fabricated endorsements even with an AI label (3P). FTC Consumer Reviews and Testimonials rule (16 CFR Part 465, effective 2024-10-21) bars fake reviews including AI-generated ones that misrepresent the reviewer or experience (V FTC press release).
- Real testimonials: genuine, consented, typical, with material connections disclosed (free product, payment). Do not condition incentives on positive sentiment.
- Synthetic persona must not claim lived sensory experience as proof (3P reading, U).
- Before/after: only real, unretouched, consented, same lighting, honest time frame, typical result. Never AI-generated or AI-enhanced results.

## 4. Cosmetic and advertising claims
- India: ASCI Code (misleading ads, substantiation), Consumer Protection Act 2019 and CCPA misleading-ad guidelines, Cosmetics Rules 2020, Drugs and Cosmetics Act (cosmetic vs drug claims). Avoid disease, treatment, cure, "dermatologically proven" or percentage claims without documented evidence. Natural, organic, chemical-free style claims need substantiation (U on exact thresholds; confirm with the regulatory lead).
- US: FTC Act section 5 and Endorsement Guides; MoCRA labelling and safety substantiation (U on details). EU/UK if relevant: claims regulation (U).
- AI visuals must not exaggerate effects (glowing skin, instant results). Show typical outcomes only. Texture and ingredient depictions must match the real product.
- Internal gate: claim list approved by the cosmetic scientist and regulatory reviewer before generation of any result-implying visual.

## 5. AI-disclosure norms
- ASCI AI labelling guidelines (released 2026-09-29, effective about three months later, U exact date): label when AI content materially influences consumer decisions: virtual influencers, replicated likeness with consent, fabricated events or settings, demos of products that do not exist; labels like "Video created using AI" or "enhanced using AI"; no label for routine edits, decorative elements, obvious fantasy, ad copy, subtitles. Prohibited regardless of label: fabricated endorsements, exaggerated results through synthetic representation, non-existent locations as real, unauthorised copyrighted work, deepfakes (3P medianama).
- Meta: "AI info" label; photorealistic AI humans in ads get a prominent label near Sponsored; disclose realistic AI content via Meta's control when no C2PA metadata (3P). Real photo slideshows are outside scope.
- YouTube: disclose realistic altered or synthetic content; May 2026 single label and auto-detection; sensitive topics (health) get prominent label; commercial ads no blanket rule but must not mislead under Google policies (3P).
- Default for our work: label any realistic AI person, AI demo or synthetic scene, in-ad where practical, plus the platform toggle.

## 6. IP and music
Original characters only; no copyrighted characters, brands, soundtracks or lyrics; props with no legible third-party text; generated audio without lyrics for ads. Keep a rights log for references.

## 7. Platform and company rules
No TikTok publishing for India work (company ads doctrine). Posting, spending and account actions need explicit user approval. Store consent forms and generation ledgers with job records.
