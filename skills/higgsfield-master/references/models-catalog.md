## Selection shortcuts
Hero realism: Veo 3.1. Product ad with refs: Seedance 2.0 then 2.5 if length needed. Volume testing: Kling 3.0 or Seedance Fast. Person stills: Soul 2.0. Text-in-image: Nano Banana Pro. Edit: Seedream or Edit Video. Always verify live availability and plan gate (Seedance 2.5 needs Pro, Plus or above, not Starter or Basic [official, 2026-10-13]; Starter gets Fast and Mini only, 3P). Nano Banana family facts: nano-banana-pro-guide.md. Official model table of help center lists Soul, Popcorn, Nano Banana, GPT Image, Seedream, Recraft, FLUX, Seedance, Kling, Wan, Grok Imagine, Sora, Veo, Hailuo, DoP; new versions become default automatically and old generations stay in Assets.
