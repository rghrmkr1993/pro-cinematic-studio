# Ads: Marketing Studio, Ads Studio, Ad Multiplier, and ad craft (researched 2026-10-13)
Labels: (V) vendor-official, (3P) third-party, (U) unverified.

## Marketing Studio (video and image, web only) (V help center)
- Flow: pick a template (1,500+; categories Product Shots, Ads, Marketplace, Posters, UGC Videos, Motion, Ref to Video), connect product (photo or URL; logo, colors, copy extracted), Generate (cost on button). Prompt path when no template fits.
- Formats: UGC (Faceless, Talking Head, Silent), Tutorial, Unboxing, Product Review, TV Spot, Hyper Motion, Wild Card, UGC Virtual Try On, Pro Virtual Try On; Motion: Hypermotion, 2D Product Motion, Mixed Media, Motion Design (for software, builds from logo or site).
- Hooks (9) and Settings (14) exist on UGC, Tutorial, Unboxing, Product Review, UGC Virtual Try On only (3P skill, snapshot 2026-05-18, lists drift): hooks Product Hit, Random Object Mic, Blizzard, Product Dodge, Spicy, Interview, Product Crash, Camera Bump, Epic Fail; settings Bedroom, Bathroom, Kitchen, Office, In Car, Street, Gym, Nature plus novelty ones. Match hook tone to brand: skincare should favour calm settings (Bathroom, Bedroom) and subtle hooks.
- Brand Kit from URL (logo, colors, fonts, imagery, tone) or manual. Soul ID spokesperson (consented) keeps one face. Ad Reference clones a reference ad's structure (rights required). Virality Predictor scores (estimate only).
- Limits: 4-15s per clip; ratios auto, 21:9, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4, 9:16; 480/720/1080p (3P); editing only in Posters (Layers); voiceover and pacing vary per generation; not in MCP or Supercomputer (V); no lip-sync for non-human characters, no multi-character consistent dialogue, no clips over 15s (3P). Escape hatches: Seedance 2.0, Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1, Cinema Studio.
- Rules: always pass an avatar for consistency, one person in avatars and extras as reference images (3P). Flowing prose under about 50 words, negate the default TV Spot packshot if unwanted. If URL extraction fails upload images.

## Ads Studio (static) (V, 2026-10-01)
URL or manual brand kit (logo, colors, fonts, tone, audience, selling points) to up to 20 ads per product per run, 4 credits each. Angles: price/offer, ingredient or material story, how used, lifestyle moment. Review each ad for claim accuracy and text errors before download.

## Ad Multiplier (V, 2026-10-06)
Upload winning reference video 4-30s plus up to 20 images; prompt with @ mentions; change product, cast, location, script, hook, CTA without re-uploading; captions removable or regenerated in another language; 480-1080p. Use for winner iteration: same angle, new hooks. Single-object changes: Genjutsu Object Swap.

## Creative frameworks that convert (craft, 3P and practitioner consensus, U on exact lift)
- UGC testimonial-style (but only real or clearly labelled synthetic creators; no fabricated customer claims): hook in 1s, problem, product moment, sensory proof, soft CTA.
- Problem to solution: show the friction (dry patch, tight skin) as a feeling, product as relief, no medical claims.
- Demo or tutorial: 3 steps, one action per beat, hands visible, texture macro.
- Before/after: only with real, unretouched, typical, consented photos and honest timelines; never AI-generated results (see compliance.md).
- Ingredient story, founder or origin story, social proof via real reviews text overlay, seasonal or offer-led.
- Unboxing and routine (GRWM) for calm natural register.
Hook formulas (0-3s): pattern interrupt (sound or motion mid-action), curiosity gap ("what I stopped using"), problem callout, bold specific claim that is substantiated, sensory macro (texture, pour, lather), POV hand reaching. First frame mid-motion, first word within 0.4s, no greetings.
Pacing: 15-30s sweet spot (3P). 9:16: cut every 1.5-3s, one idea per cut, captions burned in, CTA last 2s, loop-friendly ending. Calm brands: slower cuts (3-4s), muted grade.
Testing: one variable per variant (hook, opener, setting, CTA); creative is the main targeting lever on Meta; refresh on fatigue.

## Platform specs (3P, verify in Meta Business Help and Google Ads Help)
- Meta Reels, Stories ads: 1080x1920, 9:16, MP4 or MOV, H.264, up to 4 GB; safe zone about top 14 percent (269px), bottom 35 percent (672px), sides 6 percent (65px). Reels ad max length conflicting (90s to 15 min, U); keep 15-30s. Feed: 4:5 (1080x1350) or 1:1; 16:9 for links and video feed.
- YouTube Shorts ads: 9:16 1080x1920 recommended, 1:1 and 16:9 accepted; keep under 60s, 10-30s for action.
- Export: H.264, AAC, 30fps; burn captions inside safe zone; text over 5 percent of frame fine for video but keep legible at phone size.
- Disclosure: see compliance.md. Note: client rule in this company: no TikTok publishing for India work.

## Brand lock
One art-direction bible per brand (palette hex, one lighting rig, composition language, one grade, type as overlay only, mood). Store as Brand Kit and as a 10-12 line text bible pasted verbatim into every prompt.
