# Higgsfield tools A to Z (researched 2026-10-13)
Labels: (V) vendor-official, (3P) third-party, (U) unverified or conflicting. Credits: about 20 credits per USD for plan math, Auto-Refill $1 = 18 credits [official, 2026-10-13]. Costs show on the Generate button before confirming (V); numbers here are indicative.

## Ad Multiplier (Ads Studio, video) (V, changelog 2026-10-06)
Rebuilds a reference video ad with our product, cast, location, hook or CTA, keeping edit, motion, pacing, audio. Inputs: reference video 4-30s, up to 20 images, prompt with @ mentions. Output 480p/720p/1080p. Also via MCP. Use: scale a proven winner. Failure: reference rights, real people in reference need consent, captions may need regeneration.

## Ads Studio (static) (V, 2026-09-30)
Website URL or manual brand kit to up to 20 static ads per product per run, 4 credits each (about $0.20). Angles: offer-led, ingredient story, how-used, lifestyle. Static only; video goes to Marketing Studio. See ads-studio.md.

## AI Influencer (V, rebuilt 2026-10-03)
Game-style builder, 19 menu settings, character type Average/Bold/Extreme, optional own face (consent required), Randomize, Recreate, save as Element. 1.12 credits per character (portrait plus full body). Motion tab runs Genjutsu. Five free generations per user at launch (V changelog). Builder: Face, Body, Style tabs, types include mammals, reptiles, fish, hybrids, aliens, up to 4K; text edits cost credits; Motion Control with your own recorded movement is the official animation route; consent for a real person's movement is not addressed by the article [official, 2026-10-13]. Turn into consistent series by training Soul ID on a batch of its portraits.

## Apps (V)
Supercomputer builds and deploys interactive apps and sites from a brief (logic, storage, public link). Apps section in navigation lists them. Three templates (App detail, Preset, Studio), one-click publish to a subdomain; end users sign in with their own Higgsfield accounts and spend their own credits [official, 2026-10-13]. Treat as prototypes; review code and data handling before any business use (U on production readiness).

## Audio / Voice (V)
Audio tool: Text to Speech, Voice Change, Translate (18 languages), custom voice clone (MP3 or WAV sample, own or permitted voices only). Models: Seed Audio, Eleven (v4 added 2026-09-28, 90+ languages, 10k chars), MiniMax Speech, Seed Speech, VibeVoice, Qwen. Available via Claude MCP, not ChatGPT.

## Canvas (V)
Node board chaining prompts, references, generations (image to video to edit), parallel runs, side-by-side compare, live team editing, reusable templates, Figma/FigJam plugin. Building nodes is free; every generation deducts credits, no Unlimited [official, 2026-10-13]. Seedance: write each reference's role in the prompt. Kling: a connected image is a START FRAME, create an Element and cite @name for character refs.

## Cinema Studio (V) see cinema-studio.md
Versions 2.5, 3.0, 3.5, 4.0 (default, runs on Seedance 2.5). 4.0: 30s clips, 50 refs, up to 1080p, Director's Panel, Extend, Edit Video [official, 2026-10-13]. Claude can draft prompts and settings but never clicks Generate.

## Clipify / Personal Clipper (V, 3P)
YouTube video to clips with subtitles; Personal Clipper skill finds the strongest moment. Respect source rights.

## DoP (V)
Higgsfield's own image-to-video model with camera and effect presets (Effects, Basic/Epic Camera Control, Catch the Pulse). 3 or 5s clips, seed and steps in Advanced, Enhance toggle. Needs a sharp composed image. Best for quick VFX and preset moves.

## Draw to Edit (V, confirmed)
Official blog (2026-09-16, read 2026-10-13) describes Draw to Edit inside Seedance 2.5 Edit: mark the exact region on the frame, then describe what happens inside it; up to 50 refs, video up to 30 s, up to 1080p [official, 2026-10-13]. Also in Adobe and DaVinci plugins. Genjutsu Object Swap is the choice when the change maps to one named object. "Draw-to-Video" as a separate product is still unverified (U).

## Edit tools: Edit Image, Inpaint, Edit Video, Relight, Reframe, Expand/Outpaint (V)
Edit Video via Kling 3.0 Omni Edit, FLUX 3 Video Edit, Seedance 2.5 Edit (region marking, audio change, 480-720p). Reframe resizes video (9:16, 16:9, 4:3, 3:4, 21:9, 1:1, subject tracking); Expand outpaints images. Relight listed in changelog (details U). Use these instead of regenerating.

## Faceless Studio / Faceless Video (V, 3P)
Standalone tool (episodes up to 5 min) and Supercomputer Faceless channel (up to 15 minutes, 12 styles) [official, 2026-10-13 Supercomputer help]; the 10 min figure is superseded. 12 visual styles, AI script, voiceover, 2D animation, music, subtitles.

## Genjutsu (V, 2026-08-31, Restyle 09-30)
Motion Transfer (move a clip's motion, camera, timing onto new cast or world) and Object Swap (replace element, rest unchanged). Restyle: 20+ animation styles. Input: one video 4-30s [official, 2026-10-13: up to 30 s], up to 30 reference images, optional prompt or 30+ presets. 480p/720p/1080p; 15s clip costs 40/104/144 credits. API $0.159-$0.816 per second (3P). Use: wardrobe or product swaps, ad variants, repurposing phone footage.

## Katana (V, 2026-10-08)
Claude skill that recreates a reference edit (Aura Farming, Motion, Clipping styles) from up to 5 photos via MCP, returns MP4.

## Kling (V) see models-catalog.md
Motion Control: character image (arms and hands visible) plus clean reference video without cuts, 3-30s; transfers motion, timing, expression.

## Lipsync Studio / Speak (V)
Video menu. Upload image or video, audio text or audio, pick model and scene template (General, Selfie, Podcast, Car Talking). Image-to-video: Veo 3.1, Kling 2.6 Lipsync, Wan 2.5 Speak, Kling Avatars 2.0, Higgsfield Speak 2.0, Infinite Talk. Video-to-video: Kling Lipsync, Sync Lipsync 3 (up to 4K). Use front-facing image; covered mouths, extreme angles, fast head moves hurt sync. Native audio (Seedance, Kling 3.0, Veo, Wan 2.5) is simpler when the clip is generated fresh.

## Marketing Studio (V) see ads-studio.md
1,500+ templates, URL or photo in, 4-15s ad out. Web only, not MCP or Supercomputer, edits only in Posters (Layers) [official, 2026-10-13].

## Popcorn (V)
Storyboard and consistent multi-frame model. Modes: Manual, Auto, Instadump (photo set from your photo plus style pack up to 15 refs). Up to 8 frames, up to 4 image refs, refer by number ("man from image one"). Prompt: Subject, Setting, Style, Action, Atmosphere. Style keywords first if style drifts. Feed frames to Kling or Veo as start frames.

## Presets / Visual Effects (V)
70+ one-shot effects keyed to a start image (e.g. Earth Zoom, Orbit 360, Melting, Selfception, Smash and grab). Cheap viral formats; check brand fit and claim risk.

## Shorts Maker / Shorts Studio (V, 3P)
Supercomputer skill: topic plus hook line to a one-idea explainer short. Names vary across pages (Shorts Maker vs Shorts Studio, U).

## Soul family (V) see characters-and-consistency.md
Soul 2.0 (presets, Moodboards, Soul ID, HEX palette, 9:16 to 16:9, 1.5k/2k, batch 1-4), Soul Cinema (no presets, cinematic built in), Soul Cast (AI actor builder in Cinema Studio, about $5 lock fee, 3P), Soul Location (empty environments), Soul ID (train on 20 to 80 photos [official, 2026-10-13], one person per ID, shared across Soul, Soul 2.0 and Soul Cinema; cost not published officially, 25 credits is 3P; a few minutes).

## 3D Jutsu (V)
Browser 3D workspace: prompt or reference to editable 3D scene (objects, layout, lighting, cameras, animation) to video; GLB import and export, MP4 or still. Edit single objects without full regeneration. Older generate_3d tool makes models from an image.

## Upscale (V)
Topaz and ByteDance upscalers; FLUX 3 Video Upscaler (Precise keeps detail, Creative adds detail), up to 4K 60fps (V). Upscale last, on the selected final. Creative mode can alter faces and labels: re-run QA.

## Virality Predictor (V, in Marketing Studio)
Scores a generated video for engagement and retention. Estimate only, not a guarantee.

## Websites (V)
Supercomputer and the `websites` CLI skill build sites from a brief. Review all output before publishing.

## Plugins (V)
Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve plugins (Reframe, Draw to edit, generation). Production Skills Bundle: 11 MCP workflows for Blender, Adobe, TouchDesigner, DaVinci (2026-09-23).

## Common failure modes (all tools)
Camera ignored (use specific terms), character drift (anchors or Elements), jerky cuts (first/last frame), gibberish text, phantom third hand, product duplication, protected-content block (remove real faces or IP), stuck jobs (refresh; support if over 10 hours for Kling, V), Seedance provider outages on 2026-09-30 and 10-03 (V, refunded).
